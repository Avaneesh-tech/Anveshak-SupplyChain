Transparent by Design

Understand the journey of every product through our integrated blockchain system.

01

Farmer Integration

Farmers register crops and harvest details. Every entry is geo-tagged and timestamped on the blockchain to ensure proof of origin.

02

Processing & Manufacturing

Batch data is updated as it moves through processing units. IoT sensors monitor temperature, humidity, and quality parameters.

03

Logistics & Distribution

Real-time tracking of shipments. Any handoff between logistics providers is recorded as a new block on the ledger.

04

Consumer Verification

The final product features a unique QR code. Consumers scan it to see the complete, unalterable history of the product.
